## Status values

- DRAFT
- UNDER REVIEW
- VALIDATED
- SUPERSEDED
- REJECTED

## Rules

- Do not treat discussion as a validated decision.
- Do not invent dates, owners, or approvers.
- Link material decisions to the requirements, rules, tests, or changes they affect.
- Preserve superseded decisions for auditability.
