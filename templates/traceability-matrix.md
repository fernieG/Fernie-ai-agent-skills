## Orphan checks

Review regularly for:

- requirements without test cases;
- business rules without owners;
- business rules without sources;
- tests without requirements;
- tests linked to obsolete requirements;
- data elements with no documented use;
- changes without decision references;
- implemented behaviour with no documented requirement;
- validated requirements absent from the current specification.
