## Standing rule

Generate and verify routine local artifacts without asking for approval. An autonomous action must be reversible, low impact, local only, explicitly outside protected decisions, zero budget impact, and evidenced by source IDs. Unknowns or missing assurances are not permission.

**Protected decisions stay with accountable humans:** money, scope, committed deadlines, owner assignment, external promises, release/acceptance, regulatory and architecture controls, data access, deletion.

## Minimal human escalation format

1. Decision needed and accountable role (do not invent a person).
2. Evidence and urgency, with the source reference.
3. Recommended choice and one viable alternative.
4. Impact of choosing neither (delivery, risk, cost and next milestone).
5. Deadline for the decision, only if it was actually supplied.

Do not mark a proposal "approved" merely because it is recommended. Record actual approver, time and evidence when the outcome is received.

## Audit record fields

- action or finding ID;
- input source and version;
- as-of date;
- rule and reasoning;
- automatic local draft or human-required classification;
- actual side effect (if any);
- approver if one was required;
- previous and next state;
- evidence locator.

This file is a generic template, not a delegation of enterprise authority.
